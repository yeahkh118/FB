Executive summary

Investment planning draft

The proposed business will launch as a lean, production-led food and beverage operation with a focused menu and a controlled cost base. The initial model prioritizes takeaway, direct delivery and catering, supported by digital ordering and disciplined recipe costing. The first phase will use a USD 20,000 capital envelope to establish the operating site, essential equipment, opening inventory, brand assets and cash reserves.

The company will use its first six months to test demand, refine the menu, establish product-level contribution margins and build operating routines. Once demand and production utilization are demonstrated, the business will add recurring corporate and B2B supply. A dedicated central kitchen will be considered only when recurring volume, delivery economics, food-safety controls and available expansion capital support the investment.

Strategic objectives

What the capital must prove
01 · VALIDATE

Prove that a defined customer segment will repeatedly purchase a focused menu at prices that generate positive contribution after food, packaging and channel costs.

02 · SYSTEMIZE

Build repeatable recipes, purchasing, stock control, cash management, production planning and food-safety routines that do not depend on one individual.

03 · SCALE

Expand through catering and B2B accounts first; invest in a central kitchen only when contracted or repeat demand supports facility and equipment utilization.

Stage-gate criteria for central kitchen

  • Recurring orders from multiple customers or outlets are documented.
  • Capacity utilization and contribution per batch are measured.
  • Delivery routes remain profitable after labor, fuel and returns.
  • Food safety, traceability, labeling and shelf-life systems are validated.
  • Facility layout, equipment, utilities and staffing are quoted.
  • Expansion funding does not consume the operating cash reserve.
  • A separate central-kitchen financial case is approved by management.
